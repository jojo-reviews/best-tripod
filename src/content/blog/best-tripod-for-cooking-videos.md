The best tripod for cooking videos is an overhead support that holds the lens straight down over the counter: the Arkon Remarkable Creator Pro for a phone, the Glide Gear OH100 for a camera. For side and 45-degree angles from the floor, a tripod with a swing-out center column, such as the Manfrotto 055, covers both.

I have been shooting since 2017, and my own support kit is carbon fiber tripods, ball heads and Arca-Swiss plates, built to hold a camera upright. A recipe video asks for the opposite: a lens hanging face down over the chopping board, beside steam and messy hands. So I compared five supports on specs and named owner reviews: the [Arkon Remarkable Creator Pro](amzn:ARKON+Mounts+Overhead+Phone+Mount+Remarkable+Creator+Pro+HD8RV29), the [Glide Gear OH100](amzn:Glide+Gear+OH100+overhead+table+top+mount+stand), the [Manfrotto 055](amzn:Manfrotto+MT055XPRO3+aluminum+tripod), the [Manfrotto 190XPRO kit](amzn:Manfrotto+190XPRO+aluminum+3-section+tripod+kit+ball+head+MK190XPRO3-BHQ2) and the [Vanguard Alta Pro 2+](amzn:Vanguard+Alta+Pro+263AB+G2). ## Cooking Video Tripod Problems and Fixes

**Most cooking video tripod problems come from weight hanging outside the legs, too little height, and a cheap arm holding a phone flat.**

### A tripod standing on the counter tips

Standing a tripod on the table for height is a recurring mistake in r/videography threads. Keep it on the floor and follow a tip from r/synthesizers: "Align the boom arm over a tripod leg, and put the weight on that leg."

### Cheap overhead phone tripods break

Posters in r/videography and r/foodphotography ask for top-down rigs after a budget arm broke. Use a stand built for it like the Arkon, or a [phone mount for a tripod](/blog/best-phone-mount-for-tripod/) on floor legs.

### A horizontal column has no counterweight hook

A Photography Stack Exchange answer on Manfrotto columns calls out "the serious defect of not putting hooks at the non-camera end of the center column". My fix is one leg under the camera.

### The overhead rig is too low for a wide shot

Eric's wish is common with tabletop rigs. Mount the Glide Gear sled above the crossbar, or move to a floor tripod.

## How to Choose a Tripod for Cooking Videos

**Choose a tripod for cooking videos by the angle first, then the height over the food, then the locks.**

1. Pick the angle: overhead needs a stand, a platform or a swing-out column.
2. Measure counter to lens: countertop rigs give 12 to 29 in.
3. Match the device: a clamp for the phone's width, or a load rating above the camera and lens.
4. Choose flip locks for messy hands and a ball head to point down.
