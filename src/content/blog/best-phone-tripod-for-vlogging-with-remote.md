## How to Choose a Vlogging Phone Tripod With a Remote

**Choose a vlogging phone tripod by where you will film first, then check the remote, the phone mount and the height.** We think through each one in this order.

### Rigid extendable vs flexible legs

A phone tripod for vlogging with remote comes in two shapes. An extendable tripod or selfie stick tripod such as the KraftGeek or SENSYNE gets you to standing height and doubles as a handheld stick for walking shots. A flexible tripod in the style of the Joby GorillaPod, like the UBeesize, packs smaller and grips odd surfaces, but it never reaches eye level by itself.

### Remote type, range, and battery

A cell phone tripod with remote control is only as useful as its remote. Every Bluetooth shutter remote here is listed around 30 ft, and the KraftGeek remote is the only one with a published battery type (USB-C rechargeable). Battery details for the other two remotes are not listed, so check the product page or the box for the battery type and pack a spare if it uses a replaceable one. In practice, owner reports of dropped connections suggest you stay well inside the listed range.

### Clamp size and case compatibility

A spring phone clamp is the jaw that grips the sides of your phone, and its width limits which phones and cases fit. The SENSYNE holder is listed for phones 2.8 to 5.7 in wide. The UBeesize clamp width is unlisted, so measure a Pro Max size phone in its case before you rely on it. The KraftGeek skips the clamp entirely and uses a MagSafe mount, which reviewers say holds through cases.

### Height and load for lights and mics

A ring light and microphone on a cold shoe make a phone rig heavier and more top-heavy, which is exactly when a light stick tripod starts to sway. Only the KraftGeek has a published figure, and that 2.5 kg rating covers the magnetic mount, not the whole tripod. Treat any rating as a ceiling, not a target, and never load past it. For standing height, our guide on [how tall a tripod should be for your height](/blog/how-tall-should-a-tripod-be-for-my-height/) walks through the eye-level math.

## How to Pair the Remote and Set Up Your Vlogging Shot

**Pair the Bluetooth remote in your phone's Bluetooth settings, open the camera, and test one clip before you walk away from the tripod.** Exact steps vary by remote, so follow the manual that comes with yours.

### Pairing steps

Follow these steps the first time you connect:

1. Charge or fit the battery in the remote, then switch it on or hold its button until the light blinks.
2. Open Bluetooth on your phone and select the remote from the device list.
3. Open your camera app, switch to video, and press the remote once to start and once to stop.
4. If your app (such as Instagram or TikTok) does not respond, record in the native camera app instead and upload later.

### Framing at eye level

None of these tripods has a center column, so there is no extra height to add beyond the listed maximum. The catch is the slim top section: both tall picks wobble at full extension, so we would collapse the top section or two and stand back a little. Spread the legs fully, keep the tripod out of gusts, and hang nothing from the pole. If you later move up to a mirrorless camera, our [travel tripod for mirrorless camera guide](/blog/best-travel-tripod-for-mirrorless-camera/) covers proper load ratings and Arca-Swiss plates, which none of these phone tripods use.

### Portrait vs landscape

TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts want portrait; long YouTube videos want landscape. The UBeesize ball head and the KraftGeek magnetic holder with 360 degree rotation both switch orientation quickly. Owners report the UBeesize wobbles more in vertical, so tighten the ball head firmly before you record.

## Common Remote and Stability Problems

**Most remote problems are pairing or distance issues, and most stability problems come from full extension, wind or a loose joint.** This list covers what owners report and the quick fixes we would try first:

- **Remote will not connect:** forget the device in Bluetooth settings, re-pair, and check the battery or charge.
- **Remote drops out mid-clip:** move closer; UBeesize owners report drop-outs at longer distances.
- **Selfie toggle misfires:** SENSYNE owners report an unreliable double-tap toggle, so switch cameras on the phone itself.
- **Wobble at full height:** collapse the top section and widen your stance; both tall picks show this.
- **Joints loosen:** retighten the UBeesize ball head and legs before each session.
