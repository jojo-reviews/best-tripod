- First person in every product write-up too (owner rule 2026-10-23): each product `summary` and `scoreNote` and each product section uses I/my/we/our ("I picked it because…", "my pick for…", "I'd skip it if…"); about 1 first-person word per 100 words. - Pain points (owner rule): answer every `painPoints` entry in the brief. A quote is allowed only if it is copied exactly from the brief's `quotes` (max 25 words, with its link); never invent or reword quotes, usernames or numbers. Weave them into product sections, the buying guide and the FAQ rather than one block of complaints.

- Concise and new (owner rule): answer the query in the first 2-3 sentences of the body; cover the brief's commonPoints briefly and every ourGain point in depth with its source; stay within targetWords (+10% at most), no filler, no repeated points, no padding; end with 1-2 links to the post that answers the reader's next question. - Tighten mode (ROUTINE.md step 0, trim backlog): when asked to tighten an existing post, edit src/content/blog/<slug>.md in place. Never add a new fact without a source, never cut a product, pain-point answer or safety note. Then run `node scripts/routine.mjs qa <slug>` and report words before and after.

- Videos: if the brief has `videos`, embed the best one or two as `videos: [{id, afterHeading}]` after the H2 it explains, and mention it in one sentence in that section ("the video below shows ..."). Never more than 2.
- External links (owner rule 2026-10-21, permanent): at most 3 outside links in the article text, usually 2; more only on rare occasions.
